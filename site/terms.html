PLATFORM EXPECTATIONS

Terms of use.

These draft terms reflect the operating rules in the supplied website brief. They are not the final contract for a live marketplace and should be reviewed before launch.

1. Preview listings

All gigs and profile cards on this package are examples. They are not real vacancies or verified musician profiles. The preview does not accept applications, create accounts, send emails, confirm bookings, accept funds or pay musicians.

2. Registration and verification

A future musician account requires a verified identity, an active contact method, a performance video of at least one minute, a four-digit PIN as described in the brief, specialty information and payout setup. The platform should display the verified name returned by an authorised identity provider; it must not treat a typed NIN as proof of identity. Employers register with email and password and must complete email OTP verification. Administrator accounts are invitation-only and may be created or removed only by the owner.

3. Gig requirements

Gig requests include specialty, payment amount, location/address, GPS coordinates, start time and end time. The stated minimum gig payment is ₦2,000. The employer separately pays a ₦1,000 penalty reserve. A gig should not become publicly available until payment is confirmed by the payment provider.

4. Cancellation and scheduling

The supplied rule permits cancellation only when at least 48 hours remain before the event. A musician should only see gigs matching their specialty. An accepted gig must be added to the musician's schedule; overlapping bookings must be prevented by server-side transactional checks.

5. Attendance, fees and payout rules

The supplied brief specifies a 1% platform fee, a 10% share for the musician who posted a gig (calculated from the gig payment, not the reserve), refund of the ₦1,000 reserve if no penalty applies, a ₦1,000 deduction/refund if a musician arrives more than 30 minutes late, a ₦5,000 fine for a musician who accepts but does not attend, and an additional ₦1,000 payment to the musician if the employer misses the stated 30-minute grace rule. These amounts must be implemented through a verified payment ledger with clear evidence, refunds, and a dispute route. No funds are moved by this preview.

6. Ratings, reviews and moderation

The supplied brief requires employers to rate each musician who attended a gig. The public should not see the reviewer's identity. Admins may review new skill videos, suspend musicians, receive complaints and disclose them to the owner through a logged, permission-controlled workflow.

7. Disputes and service availability

Before launch, define provider-specific payment timing, proof of attendance, penalty appeal, refunds, service availability, intellectual-property permissions for uploaded videos and photos, account suspension appeal, governing law, and support contact details. Resolve any ambiguity in the source rules before automating transfers.

Launch requirement: these draft terms are not a substitute for a reviewed platform agreement. Do not use them to accept paid bookings until the backend and provider workflows are complete.
