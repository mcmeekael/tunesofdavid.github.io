PRIVACY & DATA

Privacy notice.

This is a starter notice for the website preview, not a final legal policy. It must be reviewed and completed before the platform accepts real users.

1. Preview mode

This ZIP contains a static website preview. The login and registration forms do not send information to a Tunes of David backend and do not save the entered form values in browser storage. Do not enter a real National Identification Number (NIN), bank account number, PIN, password, or other sensitive information.

2. Data that a future live service may need

A live version may process contact details, musician profile information, performance videos, identity-verification results, booking schedules, reviews, complaints, payment references, and payout-provider tokens. The platform should collect only what is needed for a stated purpose and explain how long it is retained.

3. Identity and payout information

The production service must use an authorised identity-verification provider. It should avoid storing raw NIN values and bank account numbers when a provider reference or token is sufficient. Private identity, contact and payout details must never be placed in public profile responses or public source code.

4. Access, security and retention

Before launch, publish the actual operator/contact details, purposes and lawful basis for processing, data-sharing information, retention periods, user-rights process, complaint channel, incident response, and applicable jurisdiction-specific information. Keep access to identity data and administrative actions audited.

5. External services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts when the browser has network access. A live platform may additionally use hosting, email, identity verification, private video storage, payment, banking, and error-monitoring providers. The final notice must identify the real providers and describe the processing they perform.

6. Contact

Add a monitored privacy contact address and a responsible organisation/operator identity before launch. No contact details are invented in this preview.

Launch requirement: replace this starter notice with a reviewed, accurate privacy notice before collecting real personal, identity, video, contact or banking information.
